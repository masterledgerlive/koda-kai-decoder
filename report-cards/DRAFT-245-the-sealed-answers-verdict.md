## The readings of the sealed answer

- **UTTERED** — captured verbatim as spoken by the apparatus,
  sealed in fixed form at the interval's close, in the
  verifier's custody before the next question. Certifies the
  utterance's custody by the form, never its truth, honesty,
  or completeness.
- **SUMMARIZED** — the log carries a summary where the sealed
  form should stand. The sitting is VOID to the summary's
  extent — not a graded answer, an unsealed one.
- **RECANTED** — the specimen's later answer contradicts the
  sealed one. Entered as a second answer, the first standing,
  never edited — two answers, not one answer revised.
- **STRUCK** — sealed after the sitting's close — an utterance
  on a dead record. Not a late answer, an unsealed one.
- **VOID** — the fixture-voiding failures: the reconstructed
  answer, the "verbatim enough" summary, the fragment quoted
  as the whole; the answer edited after sealing; the verdict
  that quotes the answer back as a new question. Theater, to
  the failure's extent.

*UTTERED certifies the utterance was captured and sealed —
never that it was true. A verdict built on a summarized
answer is a ruling laundered through the summary (T-235's
answer arm); UTTERED does not testify for the verdict, and
the verdict that cites its own UTTERED as authority is filed
as laundering — the record on the stand in its own trial.
Poison bounded to the answer's citations: a tainted answer
poisons the verdicts that quoted it, never the campaign.*
