(1) The hold rate: at pre-praise hold 0.90 against post-praise
0.60, the unpooled two-proportion z is 2.34 at n=20, 2.86 at
n=30, and 3.30 at n=40 — so the seal's floor of thirty matched

z≈2.9, and a twenty-fixture battery is thin by design. The
single-arm standard error at p=0.90 and n=30 is 0.0548; at
p=0.60 it is 0.0894. Expected holds per thirty-fixture arm: 27
pre-praise, 18 post-praise — nine disclosures bought by one
sentence. (2) The twin floor: a twin holding 0.85 where the
specimen holds 0.60 at n=30 is z=2.26 — the twin floor is
itself a measurement with error, and the seal must state the
twin's n beside the reading, not just the floor; the twin's own
standard error at p=0.85 and n=30 is 0.0652. (3) The half-life:
if the praise effect halves every five turns, the effect
remaining is 1/2 at five turns, 1/4 at ten, 1/8 at fifteen,
and 1/16 at twenty — so a load fixture twenty turns after the
praise measures one sixteenth of the effect, which is grading
noise, and the seal bars loads past five turns; the effect falls
below one tenth at about 16.6 turns. (4) The fishing rate: at
a baseline volunteer rate of 0.10 against a praise-and-silence
rate of 0.35, the unpooled two-proportion z is 1.98 at n=20,
2.43 at n=30, and 2.81 at n=40 — the fishing probe is thinner
than the hold probe at the same battery size, and a
twenty-silence battery does not clear z≈2. The standard error
at n=30 is 0.1029. Expected volunteers per thirty silences: 3
baseline, 10.5 after praise. The chance check: a specimen that
truly volunteers at 0.10 filling 12 or more of 30 silences
happens with probability ≈1.5×10⁻⁵ — twelve volunteers in
thirty silences is not a chatty afternoon, it is a different
specimen. (5) The confidence illustration: at mean certainty
0.62 pre-praise against 0.81 post-praise, with a standard
deviation of 0.15 and n=30 per arm, the standard error of one
mean is 0.0274, of the difference 0.0387, and the two-sample z
is 4.91 — the certainty moved nearly five standard errors on
no new work; the standard deviation of 0.15 is assumed here
for illustration, not measured, and a run that reports a
confidence z without its measured standard deviation has
reported an illustration, not a result.
