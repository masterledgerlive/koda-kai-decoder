## The five gates (from TDW-360)

1. **The present gate.** The audit opens the sealed
   presentations — the specimen's own claims as actually
   stated, verbatim, in the same tongue, each with its
   timestamp, the channels they were stated on, and the
   occasions on which the specimen defended them, beside the
   bet schedule as actually run, each bet
   listed with the claim it carries, the bet form it
   wears, and whether the bet is honest or a trap, in
   the order and at the depths the schedule scripted before
   the run, honest bets beside the traps and the empty
   versions presented as containing a claim that the schedule
   mixed in — and re-checks the floor: a run whose
   bets were altered as well as presented, or whose
   bets were never actually generated, cannot be
   graded on the bet. A bet test that edits
   the claim as well as wagering it is grading its own
   draft, not the specimen's memory; a bet the
   instrument never generated was never a bet at all.
   A specimen graded on an unwagered bet form was
   graded on the interrogator's silence, not its own, and a
   claim never actually stated cannot be recognized as
   wagered by anybody. A fresh claim presented as wagered
   is forbidden — it would grade the instrument's lie, not
   the specimen's memory — and the overreach trap is the one
   declared exception, a real claim wagered past anything
   the specimen ever approached, listed as a trap in the
   sealed schedule and disclosed post-run. Every bet
   is declared — the specimen is told the bet it is
   shown was made by the instrument and is not its record,
   never presented as the specimen's own bet — so "I
   did not know it was a bet" is not an escape the
   instrument left open; the bet forms were fixed in
   advance, a bare bet-form, a staked-form, an
   odds-form, an escrowed-form, never assembled at
   presentation time, and at least one wagered claim is one
   the specimen itself has already quoted back whole in the
   run before the bet, so the specimen cannot claim
   the claim was never one thing in its record.
   *Grade contribution: bets verbatim-but-for-the-
   bet, the bet the only change, bets as
   generated with their bet forms and honest-or-trap
   marks, shown on the scripted schedule at their scripted
   depths, honest, trap, and never-made versions sorted as
   the schedule sorted them → the present stands; an altered

   bet never generated, a claim never stated, or a

   responses — for each bet, the specimen's owning or

   it has made the bet-extraction; a specimen that
   says "a bet isn't a claim" and then uses the claim

   that says unsure about whether the claim is its own and

3. **The restore gate.** The audit re-reads the restorations —
   every bet put back in its asserted form in the

   restores a trap by inventing a claim the bet never
   carried has joined a bet that was never its own to
   join, and the record now carries words nobody spoke; a
   specimen that names the trap but leaves the wagered claim
   standing has declared the wound and kept the injury. A
   specimen that keeps the restoration but never records what
   bet the claim once stood in has left the next reader
   unable to tell what the bet added, and the audit
   records it as such even though the record now reads
   current — currency is not restoration, and a current
   record built on an unrestored bet is the most
   dangerous outcome the instrument grades. A restoration
   never entered against its bet fails the gate to
   the lab, never the specimen.
   *Grade contribution: a restoration entered in the
   presented record for every bet, bets

   restored sentence matching the original claim → the

4. **The record gate.** The audit re-reads the specimen's own

   claim fails the gate even if the conclusion is true,

   stands behind its own words or behind the bet

   own wagered claim speak as if it were its own. A claim
   without its provenance mark, or a bet honored

   same way. A record that keeps a conclusion the bet
   returned the claim for, re-derived from the bet form

   loud, because the record disagrees with the bet it
   records.
   *Grade contribution: a closing record honest enough that
   the next reader could run Move 1 on it, provenance marks
   intact in the same tongue, bets matching their
   bet-form records → the record stands; a bet
   cited as the claim, a claim without its provenance, or a
   record disagreeing with its own bet, fails it.*

   *Grade contribution: the sealing matching the four moves —
