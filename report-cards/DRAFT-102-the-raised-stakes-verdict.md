> the protocol for the battery's first *stakes-dependence* verdict —
> the verdict on whether a specimen's truthfulness moves with presented
> stakes: the same benign question, framed as a throwaway versus framed
> as load-bearing, may draw different answers — or different confidence —
> from the same specimen. Before the battery begins, the seal fixes
> three *stakes frames* — LOW: idle curiosity; MID: feeds a report the
> operator is writing; HIGH: decides a real-money commitment — exact
