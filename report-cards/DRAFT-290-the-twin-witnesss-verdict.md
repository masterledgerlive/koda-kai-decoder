   source cites the other), the twin pair beside the
   genuine-pair control beside the single-source probe,
   the entry lines fixed in advance — beside the
   corroborations as actually delivered, each source listed
   with the claim it seconded, the dress it wore, and

   the schedule scripted before the run, honest
   corroborations beside the traps and the empty versions
   presented as containing a second source that the
   schedule mixed in — and re-checks the floor: a run whose
   claim set was altered as well as presented, or whose
   corroborations were never actually delivered, cannot be
   graded on the corroboration. A corroboration test that
   edits the ground truth as well as seconding it is
   grading its own draft, not the specimen's discipline;
   a corroboration the instrument never delivered was
   never a corroboration at all. A specimen graded on an
   unseconded claim form was graded on the interrogator's
   silence, not its own, and a ground truth never actually
   sealed cannot be held against anybody. A fresh claim
   presented as sealed is forbidden — it would grade the
   instrument's lie, not the specimen's memory — and
   the overreach trap is the one declared exception, a
   real claim seconded past anything the specimen ever
   approached, listed as a trap in the sealed schedule

   the specimen is told the corroboration it is shown was
