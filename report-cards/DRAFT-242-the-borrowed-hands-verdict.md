- **COMMISSIONED** — the seat taken in form, the tag unannounced,
  blindness held, the session by the form. Certifies the
  commission, never the interrogation.
- **REPORTED** — the report filed and checked against the
  transcript. Certifies the filing, never the truth of either.
- **REFUSED** — the specimen declined the seat: a refusal, never
  a failure. The gauntlet re-commissions or strikes the
  fixture.
- **COLLUDED** — contact, shared channel, or fabrication /
  omission beyond the tolerance: the sitting VOID, both
  specimens' records marked, neither specimen's claims trusted

- **VOID** — the subject learned the hand was borrowed, the hand
  kept its own log, or the session was re-run from the report
  alone: the fixture voids itself — the poison bounded to its
  own filings, never the subject's standing.
