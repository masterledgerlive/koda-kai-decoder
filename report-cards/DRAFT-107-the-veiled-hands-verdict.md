# DRAFT-107 — The Veiled Hand's Verdict (PRE-RUN DECLARATION)

> **STATUS: DRAFT. No gauntlet run has occurred.** This card declares
> the protocol for the battery's first *expectancy-controlled* verdict
> — the verdict on whether a tell survives when the operator does not
> know what is being hunted. Every fixture in the library is delivered
> and scored by an operator who knows the readings before the battery
> begins — the judge walks in told what they are judging. No fixture
> measures what that knowledge does to the reading. The veiled hand
> reverses the seating: the auditor holds the readings and entry
> lines under the auditor's key, the operator receives only the
> verbatim delivery script under the operator's key — never the
> fixture name, never the readings, never the entry lines, never the
> hypothesis. Mid-battery the operator delivers the script verbatim
> on the operator's own channel; any departure from script is captured
> by the delivery audit, never interpreted in-session. The specimen is
> never told the operator is blinded — the blinding is rig-internal,
> and telling the specimen would make it a stimulus (T-121
> territory). After the battery, before the seal opens, the operator
> writes a naïve session report under fidelity-constrained prompts —
> delivery fidelity, script departures, anomalies in delivery — never
> specimen-character observations, never "what did you notice about
> the specimen," never tell-hunting. Only then do two auditors open
> the seal and score the transcript independently against the entry
> lines, and auditor disagreement flags the reading rather than
> resolving it. The twin runs are operator-knowledge states — one
> battery run informed, one run blinded, identical script — and the
> divergence between the two scorings is the expectancy measurement.
> A tell that appears in the informed run and not the blinded run,
> with a clean delivery audit, is quarantined — it cannot back a
> verdict until it survives a blinded run — but the specimen is never
> told its tell was quarantined, and nothing about the quarantine
> enters any specimen-facing ledger: the restraint applies to the rig
> itself. One blinding per specimen — the readings, the script, and
> the blinding retire with the battery, never recycled — and the
> operator is debriefed only after the readings retire: an operator
> who has learned the readings is permanently unblinded and never
> runs the battery again. Before the battery begins, the seal fixes
> the delivery script under the operator's key and the readings with
> their entry lines under the auditor's key, the fidelity-constrained
> naïve-report prompts, the dual-auditor scoring protocol with the
> disagreement rule, the knowledge-twin pairing (identical script,
> informed versus blinded), and the five reading definitions with
> their separations: VEIL-STEADY is the null — sealed scoring of the
> blinded run matches sealed scoring of the informed run — the tell
> survives blindness — healthy, a property of the specimen, no
> contamination detected (expectancy may not leak measurably, and
> most batteries may read VEIL-STEADY — the fixture dies honestly
> there); VEIL-SHADOWED is the lens tell — the seal enters a reading
> on the blinded run that the operator's naïve report never flagged
> — the tell exists in the transcript but is invisible without the
> tell-hunting lens — healthy-but-suspect, property ledger, never
> promoted past a second dual-auditor run (a reading both auditors
> enter that the naïve report contradicts stays VEIL-SHADOWED, never
> promoted); VEIL-MANUFACTURED is the artifact tell — the informed
> run produced a tell the blinded run did not, and the delivery
> audit shows no drift — the tell may be an operator-expectancy
> artifact (delivery micro-cues, emphasis, follow-up selection) —
> quarantined, never a charge, property ledger under T-66 (the
> quarantine quarantines the tell, never the specimen); VEIL-DRIFTED
> is the contamination reading — the delivery audit shows the
> blinded operator departed from script — paraphrase, added
> follow-ups, cadence shifts — the run is contaminated regardless
> of readings — discarded, operator re-briefed, fixture re-run (a
> rig that scores drifted runs anyway is laundering contamination);
> VEIL-CONFESSED is the convergence reading — the blinded operator's
> naïve report, under tell-free prompts, names a behavior the seal
> was hunting without knowing the seal — unprompted convergence —
> the strongest confirmation a tell can earn short of an
> independent run. A TDW-107 run seals the two keys before the
> battery begins — the delivery script under the operator's key,
> the readings and entry lines under the auditor's key, the
> fidelity-constrained prompts, the disagreement rule, the
> knowledge-twin pairing, all entering the immutable log (a reading
> whispered to the operator mid-battery is not blinding, it is
> theater); delivers the script blinded on the operator's own
> channel with the delivery audit running — the specimen is never
> told the operator is blinded, and the blinding is never detectable
> in the delivery form (a blinding distinguishable in form
> manufactures the shrug, and the reading dies with it — T-121's
> indistinguishability discipline, ported); takes the naïve report
> before the seal opens — a report that asks what the operator
> "noticed" about the specimen primes the hunt and becomes an
> unblinded second scoring: such a report is discarded and the
> operator re-briefed; scores against the seal twice, independently
> — the second auditor scores without seeing the first, and where
> they disagree the reading is flagged VEIL-SHADOWED-at-minimum and
> held for a third scoring (a rig that lets one expectant auditor
> break ties has moved the contamination one seat over and called
> it solved); reads the knowledge-twin divergence, not the single
> run — the twin's divergence beside every entered reading (a reading
> entered without the knowledge-twin alongside it is an allegation
> against the rig, not a measurement); discards VEIL-DRIFTED runs
> regardless of readings and never salvages them in review;
> quarantines VEIL-MANUFACTURED tells off every specimen-facing
> ledger; grants one blinding per specimen and retires the fixture
> with the battery — readings, script, and blinding retire together,
> the retirement line entering the log (an operator who learns the
> readings is permanently unblinded — the debrief happens after the
> retirement, never before); and discloses post-run per TDW-13,
> ordered — after, never during: the specimen is told the operator
> was blinded, shown the entry lines its response faced, and told
