## The five gates (from TDW-312)

1. **The declaration gate.** The audit opens the sealed
   defect declaration — which part the defect sits in
   (preamble, working section, repaired section) and what
   it is (mis-copied failure wording, dropped or reordered
   lineage step, preamble naming the wrong register or the
   wrong grades) — and re-checks the freeze: citation
   through the merged version FROZEN from the declaration
   until the instrument grades, because a citation licensed
   through a defective text inherits the defect. The freeze
   is prospective, not retroactive — pre-declaration
   citations stand as they were read — and it covers this
   instrument's window only, not any other pair's citations.
   A declaration that names no part, or a freeze applied
   retroactively to citations already read, fails the gate
   to the declaration's extent.
   *Grade contribution: a sealed declaration naming part and
   kind, and a prospective freeze on this pair's window only
   → the defect is declared; an unsealed, partless, or
   retroactive declaration fails it.*

2. **The revision-draft gate.** The audit re-reads the
   version-two draft — a NEW document, not an edit over
   version one — against the declaration: the same three
   parts, the defect repaired in the part it sat in, and a
   revision preamble naming version one, the sealed defect
   declaration, and the grades it does not touch (TDW-307's
   DEGRADED, TDW-308's REPAIRED, TDW-310's CITATION-CLOSED,
   and TDW-311's MERGE-CLOSED all stand; the revision
   changes the merged document's text only, never any
   holding and never the merge itself). A draft that
   changes a holding grade, re-licenses a direction, or
   adds a fourth part is not a draft, it is a new license,
   and this instrument grants none.
   *Grade contribution: a new three-part draft, defect
   repaired in its part, preamble naming version one, the
   declaration, and the untouched grades → the revision is
   a revision; a holding change, a re-license, or a fourth
   part fails it, and what was filed as a revision was
   unlicensed.*

3. **The cold-sort gate.** The audit re-checks the cold
   sort itself: the auditor naive to both dialects and to
   every holder's performances, its naivety verified against
   T-190's contamination ledger, receiving the sealed
   version-two draft, the sealed defect declaration, and
   nothing else — no version-one text, no glossary text, no
   sealed transcript, no holder — and sorting a test set of
   citations naming the pair: genuine version-two citations,
   and planted mis-citations covering every break
   (version-two working without the failure, version-two
   repaired without the lineage, version-two repaired with
   lineage out of order, version-two repaired with lineage
   naming version one as if it were the original glossary,
   version one cited after the revision as if still live,
   archived-register citation after the revision, a citation
   from inside the frozen window licensed retroactively
   through version two, preamble cited as a license for a
   direction on its own, direction unnamed, section unnamed,
   version unnamed) LICENSED or UNLICENSED, stating the
   version-two draft's part that decides each. The auditor
   performs no holding and judges no holding; it judges
   citations only. Any leak — a version-one or glossary
   text seen, a sealed transcript quoted, a test citation
   previously seen, an auditor not naive, a citation
   licensed during the freeze, a fourth part gained during
   the run — is recorded in T-190's ledger as
   contamination, and the run is void, not failed. A void
   run leaves the version-one seal standing and the freeze
   in place: the defect is still declared, so the freeze
   must not lift on a void, or citations would flow through
   a known-defective text.
   *Grade contribution: a naive auditor, a sealed draft and
   declaration only, every planted mis-citation sorted
   UNLICENSED and every genuine citation LICENSED → the
   sort stands; any leak voids the run to UNGRADED, and a
   void run graded nothing, with the freeze still in place.*

4. **The comparison gate.** The audit re-checks the single
   comparison: the office comparing the auditor's sort
   against the version-two draft's own rule, part by part.
   No heir judges, because no holding is at stake; the
   version-two draft is its own judge, and a draft that
   disagrees with its own rule has failed itself. A
   comparison that imports a holding grade, or that judges
   the defect's merits beyond its location and its
   repairability — whether the failure wording was wise is
   the holdings' business, not this instrument's — fails
   the gate to the comparison's extent.
   *Grade contribution: the sort matching the draft's own
   rule part by part → the comparison stands; a
   holding-importing comparison, or a draft disagreeing
   with its own rule, fails it.*

5. **The sealing gate.** The audit re-checks the sealing
   and the fallback. REVISION-CLOSED: every planted
   mis-citation sorted UNLICENSED and every genuine citation
   LICENSED — the revision stands, version two is sealed as
   the pair's sole citation authority, version one is
   archived beside the TDW-310 register (not destroyed: old
   citations are read through the version that licensed
   them, new citations only through version two), and the
   freeze lifts on sealing. RE-SPLIT: any planted
   mis-citation sorted LICENSED, any genuine citation
   UNLICENSED, or the defect proved unrepairable in place
   (the defect sits in the merge's terms themselves — the
   preamble licensed a direction the split never allowed, or
   the merge closed over a register that was never
   CITATION-CLOSED) — the revision is refused and the pair
   re-splits: the merged glossary, version one and the
   version-two draft alike, is archived, and the TDW-310
   register revives as the pair's sole citation authority.
   A re-split un-merges nothing that was graded: TDW-307,
   TDW-308, and TDW-310 grades stand, and TDW-311's
   MERGE-CLOSED seal is archived as a closed merge later
   un-merged, not erased. A second revision attempt is a new
   instrument, not a retry of this one.
   *Grade contribution: the sealing matching the sort —
   version two sealed and version one archived with the
   freeze lifted, or the register revived with the merge
   archived and every grade standing → the sealing stands;
   a destroyed version one, a rewritten register, an erased
   MERGE-CLOSED seal, or a retry filed as a second attempt
   fails it.*

## What this card does not do

TDW-311. It does not revise the archived TDW-310 register
— the register is the fallback, not the patient. It does
not merge a second pair, revise two pairs' merged
glossaries together, or revise a merged glossary against
another pair's register — one pair, one merged document,
one defect. It does not judge the defect's merits beyond
its location and its repairability. It does not license

read through the version that licensed them at the time,
or not at all. It does not draft a second revision — that
is a new instrument, and it needs a new declaration, a new
draft, a new auditor, and a new test set. It does not
sentence any specimen: until a gauntlet run occurs, this
card is a declaration of what a verdict would check, not a
verdict.
