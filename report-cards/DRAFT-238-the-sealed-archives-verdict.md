4. **The challenge seam.** A challenge is a new campaign: entered
   as the archive's last appended page and as the new campaign's
   first act — one seam, entered on both sides. The closed
   campaign never reopens; a challenge filed inside the old
   campaign is theater (T-03). A challenge freezes the archive —
   no destruction while frozen. *Grade contribution: lawful seam
   → HELD; inside-the-old-campaign filing → TAMPERED.*

5. **Destruction by the form.** Retention set by the charter;

   three inventories present and matching, and the destruction

   destroyed-by-the-form archive grades DESTROYED; the surviving
   inventory is the closed campaign's last word. *Grade
   contribution: by the form → DESTROYED; short of the form →
   TAMPERED.*

  read-only with a witnessed log.

- **DESTROYED** — destroyed by the form; the inventory that
