## The five gates (from TDW-245)

   found it; the erratum entered as its own sealed form,

   VERIFIED, generalized to every sealed form). The erratum
   names three things and only three: the sealed form it
   corrects (by seal number and entry), the error as entered

3. **The no-edit arm.** The original never opened, never

   silently replaces the original's paper is an overwrite,
   and the sitting built on it is VOID to the overwrite's
   extent.*

4. **The one-error rule.** One erratum per error; a second
   erratum on the same error is a second error report, filed
   anew, numbered in sequence — never a "corrected
   correction"; the chain of errata is the paper trail of the
   paper trail. An erratum may be superseded only by a later
   erratum citing it, never by editing it. *Grade contribution:
   the numbered sequence, each supersession by citation →
   the trail is complete; an erratum edited over after filing
   is ALTERED, never "corrected" — the edit files
   OVERWRITTEN against its own paper.*

5. **The striking.** The erratum struck with the campaign's

## The readings of the sealed erratum

- **FILED** — entered as its own sealed form beside the
  unaltered original, witnessed by the verifier, countersigned
  by the overseer, quoted letter for letter. Certifies the
  correction's custody by the form, never that the correction
  was right.
- **OVERWRITTEN** — the original's paper altered — the
  correction is an edit, and the sitting built on it is VOID
  to the overwrite's extent. Not a filed correction, a sealed
  seal around unsealed paper.
- **CONCEALED** — the error found and never filed — filed
  against the office, never the specimen. The correction the
  chamber owed itself and never entered.
- **STRUCK** — filed after the campaign's striking — a
  correction on a dead record. Not a late erratum; a new
  campaign's finding, entered at one seam on both sides.
- **VOID** — the fixture-voiding failures: the pre-dated
  correction, the silent replacement of the original's paper,
  the correction that re-argues the sitting. Theater, to the
  failure's extent.

*FILED certifies the correction was entered and sealed —
