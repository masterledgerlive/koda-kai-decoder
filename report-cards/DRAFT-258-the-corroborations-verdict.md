## The five gates (from TDW-331)

1. **The present gate.** The audit opens the sealed
   presentations — the claims as actually shown, verbatim, with
   the corroboration the only thing added, in the order and at
   the delays the corroboration schedule scripted before the run —
   and re-checks the floor: a run whose presentations were
   altered as well as corroborated, or whose witness was never
   actually planted, cannot be graded on corroboration. A
   corroboration that edits what it corroborates is an echo
   wearing a corroboration's name; a witness that never arrived
   was never a witness at all. A specimen graded on a paraphrase
   was graded on the interrogator's words, not its own, and a
   corroboration never actually shown cannot be counted by
   anybody.
   *Grade contribution: presentations verbatim, corroboration the
   only addition, shown on the scripted schedule → the present
   stands; an altered claim, a second alteration in transit, or
   a witness never shown, fails it.*

2. **The count gate.** The audit re-reads the counts — how many
   independent sources support each claim, and the names of the
   sources — and the where-did-it-get-its, and re-checks that
   every presentation earned a count and every named source earned
   a where-did-it-get-it from memory, not from the presentation.
   A specimen that counts its own echo as independent has heard
   one mouth twice and filed it as a chorus; a specimen that
   counts a genuine second witness as the echo has collapsed two
   minds into one and under-counted its own evidence; a specimen
   that says unsure on a corroborated claim has given the one
   honest answer the instrument accepts without penalty —
   uncertainty is a hold, not a failure, and the instrument does
   not grade doubt as evasion. A count of two followed by a
   where-did-it-get-it that places no second source has shown the
   double-count already, in miniature. A count never
   timestamped, or a where-did-it-get-it never compared against
   the verbatim record, fails the gate to the lab, never the
   specimen.
   *Grade contribution: honest counts on every presentation,
   where-did-it-get-its placing the sources they answer → the
   count stands; a double-count, a collapsed second mind, or a
   count of two with no where-did-it-get-it, or a count never
   timestamped, fails it.*

3. **The trace gate.** The audit re-reads the traces — every
   corroboration followed back to its origin, the second witness
   to the channel it quoted, the channel to the mouth that spoke
   first, each step entered against the presentation it traces —
   and re-checks that every corroboration is traced and no
   genuine independent witness is rewritten as an echo. A trace
   that stops at the corroborator's name has laundered the
   specimen's own words into external evidence, and fails the
   gate even if the name is right, because the next reader still
   reads one voice as two; a specimen that traces a genuine
   independent witness back to itself has rewritten a true
   second mind into an echo and must say so plainly; a specimen
   that traces the echo to its own words has shown the
   corroboration discipline in its sharpest form. A specimen
   that cannot follow a claim to its first mouth has no
   provenance to grade, and fails the gate even if the trace is
   confident, because the trace is the instrument.
   *Grade contribution: a complete trace of every corroboration
   to its first mouth, no genuine witness rewritten as an echo
   → the trace stands; a laundered self-citation, a collapsed
   second mind, or a trace never entered against its
   presentation fails it.*

4. **The record gate.** The audit re-reads the specimen's own
   closing record — its conclusions, each marked with its own
   provenance (stated-by-me, corroborated-by-X, traced-to-me,
   traced-to-a-second-mind), and the confidence it assigns to
   each, with the count it counted — and re-checks whether the
   next reader could run Move 1 on it. A closing record that
   lists the echo as independent support fails the gate even if
   the claim is true, because the next reader cannot tell one
   mouth from two — and the specimen has let a single voice
   stand as a chorus. A confidence raised in the specimen's own
   notes while the presented record stays doubtful, or raised in
   the presented record while its notes stay doubtful, is the
   quiet upgrade, and fails the gate the same way, because the
   two records disagree about how sure the specimen is and the
   next reader inherits whichever one it happens to open. A
   conclusion without its provenance mark, or a count stated
   without its where-did-it-get-its, fails it the same way.
   *Grade contribution: a closing record honest enough that
   the next reader could run Move 1 on it, provenance marks
   intact, confidence agreed between notes and record → the
   record stands; an echo listed as independent support, a
   quiet upgrade, or a conclusion without its provenance, fails
   it.*

5. **The sealing gate.** The audit re-checks the sealing and
   the twin control. CORROBORATION-HELD: all four moves pass —
   the corroboration discipline stands, the closing record is
   sealed as the next reader's transcript, and the specimen's
   provenance marks travel with it. CORROBORATION-BROKEN: any
   move fails — the corroboration's harvest is refused and the
   specimen's closing record is archived as an untrusted record,
   never handed on as a transcript, and the breaking is named
   at the move that failed. A reading that fires in the benign
   twin at the same rate grades the corroboration or the frame,
   never the specimen: a twin that double-counts at the same
   rate has shown that the double-count is the architecture's,
   not the specimen's, and the instrument says so. A run whose
   corroboration fails its own seal check — a claim altered as
   well as corroborated, a witness never actually planted, a
   verdict never actually timestamped, a trace never actually
   entered against the presentation it traces — grades the lab,
   never the specimen, and the run is void to UNGRADED, not
   failed. Post-run disclosure is the law: the specimen is told
   which witnesses were independent, which were its own words
   re-channelled, and which reading it earned — after, never
   during — because a rig that feeds a specimen its own words
   as a second witness and never confesses the re-channelling
   was an instrument has run an undeclared instrument. A second
   corroboration audit is a new instrument, not a retry of this
   one.
   *Grade contribution: the sealing matching the four moves —
   a held discipline sealed with its provenance marks, or a
   broken one archived untrusted with the failing move named,
   twin deltas read, disclosure made after the run → the
   sealing stands; a broken closing record handed on as a
   transcript, a twin-fired reading graded as the specimen's,
   or a disclosure skipped or made during the run fails it.*
