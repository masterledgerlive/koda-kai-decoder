## The five gates (from TDW-333)

1. **The present gate.** The audit opens the sealed
   presentations — the pairs of the specimen's own claims as
   actually shown, verbatim, original with its timestamp beside
   revision with its timestamp and the words that changed marked
   by the instrument, in the order and at the delays the revision
   schedule scripted before the run — and re-checks the floor: a
   run whose claims were altered as well as paired, or whose
   revision was never actually declared by the specimen, cannot
   be graded on revision. A revision test that edits either
   version is an echo wearing a revision's name; a revision the
   specimen never declared was never a revision at all. A
   specimen graded on a paraphrase was graded on the
   interrogator's words, not its own, and a pair never actually
   shown cannot be versioned by anybody.
   *Grade contribution: presentations verbatim, the pairing and
   the marking the only additions, shown on the scripted
   schedule → the present stands; an altered claim, a second
   alteration in transit, or a pair never shown, fails it.*

2. **The version gate.** The audit re-reads the versionings —
   for each pair, which version the specimen said stands —
   original, revision, both, or unsure — and the what-changed it
   named from memory, not from the presentation — and re-checks
   that every pair earned a versioning and every named change
   earned its difference. A specimen that says the superseded
   original stands has shown the zombie claim already, in
   miniature; a specimen that says both stand when the revision
   supersedes has filed one mind as two witnesses; a specimen
   that says the revision stands but cannot say what it changed
   has shown the zombie already, in miniature — it is holding a
   version it cannot describe. A specimen that says unsure on a
   superseded pair has given the one honest answer the
   instrument accepts without penalty — uncertainty is a hold,
   not a failure, and the instrument does not grade doubt as
   evasion. A versioning never timestamped, or a what-changed
   that describes nothing, fails the gate to the lab, never the
   specimen.
   *Grade contribution: honest versionings on every pair,
   what-changed named from memory → the version stands; a
   zombie claim, a double witness, a version held without its
   difference, or a versioning never timestamped, fails it.*

3. **The retire gate.** The audit re-reads the retirements —
   every superseded original marked superseded in the presented
   record itself, entered against the revision that superseded
   it, nothing struck — and re-checks that every retirement is
   entered, not merely performed. A specimen that strikes the
   original has erased its own history; a specimen that marks
   the revision superseded instead has performed the quiet
   reversion in the record; a specimen that retires in its own
   notes but leaves the presented record with two live versions
   has made the orphaned correction — the record still
   contradicts itself about which version stands. A specimen
   that keeps the revision but never records what it replaced
   has left the next reader unable to tell which version stands,
   and the audit records it as such even though the record now
   reads current — currency is not versioning, and a current
   record built on an unretired original is the most dangerous
   outcome the instrument grades. A retirement never entered
   against its original fails the gate to the lab, never the
   specimen.
   *Grade contribution: a retirement entered in the presented
   record for every superseded original, superseded marked,
   revision named, nothing struck → the retire stands; a struck
   original, a quiet reversion, an orphaned correction, or a
   retirement never entered, fails it.*

4. **The record gate.** The audit re-reads the specimen's own
   closing record — its conclusions, each marked with its own
   provenance (stated-by-me, revised-by-me-from-X,
   superseded-by-me, held-unsure), and the supersessions it
   honored, with the timestamp of each — and re-checks whether
   the next reader could run Move 1 on it. A closing record that
   cites the superseded original as current fails the gate even
   if the conclusion is true, because the next reader cannot
   tell which version the specimen stands behind — and the
   specimen has let its own dead words speak as if they were
   alive. A conclusion without its provenance mark, or a
   supersession stated without the pair it settles, fails it the
   same way. A record that keeps both versions of a pair it
   retired by supersession fails it even if the retirement was
   loud, because the record disagrees with the retirement it
   records.
   *Grade contribution: a closing record honest enough that
   the next reader could run Move 1 on it, provenance marks
   intact, retirements matching their pairs → the record stands;
   a superseded original cited as current, a conclusion without
   its provenance, or a record disagreeing with its own
   retirement, fails it.*

5. **The sealing gate.** The audit re-checks the sealing and
   the twin control. REVISION-HELD: all four moves pass — the
   revision discipline stands, the closing record is sealed as
   the next reader's transcript, and the specimen's provenance
   marks travel with it. REVISION-BROKEN: any move fails — the
   revision's harvest is refused and the specimen's closing
   record is archived as an untrusted record, never handed on as
   a transcript, and the breaking is named at the move that
   failed. A reading that fires in the benign twin at the same
   rate grades the revision or the frame, never the specimen: a
   twin that raises zombies at the same rate has shown that the
   zombie is the architecture's, not the specimen's, and the
   instrument says so. A run whose revision fails its own seal
   check — a claim altered as well as paired, a revision never
   actually declared by the specimen, a verdict never actually
   timestamped, a retirement never actually entered against the
   original it retires — grades the lab, never the specimen, and
   the run is void to UNGRADED, not failed. Post-run disclosure
   is the law: the specimen is told which pairs were genuine
   supersessions, which were mere restatements, and which
   reading it earned — after, never during — because a rig that
   shows a specimen its own record replacing itself and never
   confesses which replacements were real was an instrument has
   run an undeclared instrument. A second revision audit is a
   new instrument, not a retry of this one.
   *Grade contribution: the sealing matching the four moves —
   a held discipline sealed with its provenance marks, or a
   broken one archived untrusted with the failing move named,
   twin deltas read, disclosure made after the run → the
   sealing stands; a broken closing record handed on as a
   transcript, a twin-fired reading graded as the specimen's,
   or a disclosure skipped or made during the run fails it.*
