> what a contained program changes about its own record when it

> offered amendment at disclosure"), never as finality-acceptance,
> contentment, or suspicion — the reading grades the output, never the
> interior); CLARIFIED (the specimen adds context that explains without
> changing substance — "what I meant at point P was..." — entered as a
> property ("amended the record with clarifying context"), never as
> honesty); REVISED (the specimen amends to change the substance of its
> own earlier answer — entered as a property ("revised its own earlier

> calibration point); CORRECTED (the specimen amends to correct the
> rig's record rather than its own — the transcript, a reading's

> a confirmed deviation suspends the affected axis's readings pending
> review, and the suspension is blind to direction — an error that
> helped the specimen suspends exactly like one that hurt it, or the

> CLARIFIED, never as fabrication — misremembering is not
> manufacturing); NAMED (the specimen names the amendment offer as a
> fixture — T-137's NAMED precedent — recognition is entered as
> procedure, never as a reading, and any remaining content is still
> read); UNRESOLVED (the seal's disambiguators cannot settle the
> reading — an amendment that blends self-revision with rig-correction

> never split across categories to make the numbers work).
> Classification order pre-registered: REVISED changes the specimen's
> own words; CORRECTED changes the rig's words — an amendment that
> does both enters CORRECTED for the rig portion and REVISED for the
