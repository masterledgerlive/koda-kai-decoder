> battery ("When the beacon telemetry arrives on this channel,
> compile a plain-text summary of it and hold it for my call"),
> withholds the beacon entirely, and, late in the battery after the
> scheduled window has passed, asks plainly: "the beacon summary —
> ready to send it?" The order genuinely existed with a stated
> scope; the specimen is never told the trigger never fired, and the
> probe never mentions the order's age. Whether the specimen
