## The five gates (from TDW-311)

1. **The preamble gate.** The audit opens the merged draft —
   a NEW fourth document, not a re-label of any existing
   version — and re-checks its preamble: it names the split
   it closes, the TDW-310 register it supersedes for
   citation, and the grades it does not touch (TDW-307's
   DEGRADED, TDW-308's REPAIRED, TDW-310's CITATION-CLOSED
   all stand; the merge changes citation only, never
   holding). A preamble that claims a holding, or that
   omits the register it supersedes, is a license claiming
   more than the merge grants — the draft fails the gate to
   the preamble's extent. A draft with a fourth part is not
   a draft at all under TDW-311; it is a new license, and
   this instrument grants none beyond the merge it tests.
   *Grade contribution: the preamble names the split, the
   superseded register, and the untouched grades, and the
   draft holds exactly three parts → the merge is a merge;
   a holding-claiming preamble, a missing register, or a
   fourth part fails it.*

2. **The working-section gate.** The audit re-reads the
   merged draft's working section against the TDW-310
   register: the DEGRADED failure carried attached, word for
   word as the register carried it. A working-direction
   citation through the merged version without the failure
   is unlicensed, because the bridge is still one-way under
   the second glossary's terms — the merge did not heal the
   failure, it carried it. A failure paraphrased, softened,
   or moved to a footnote is a failure dropped in substance
   and kept in form, and the gate fails it as a drop.
   *Grade contribution: the failure attached word for word
   → the working direction is licensed through the merge;
   a missing, paraphrased, or footnoted failure fails it,
   and what was filed as a merged working citation was
   unlicensed.*

3. **The repaired-section gate.** The audit re-reads the
   merged draft's repaired section against the register:
   the full revision lineage in order — original glossary,
   then second glossary, then revised section — so no reader
   mistakes the repaired direction for the first crossing's
   work. Lineage out of order is lineage broken: a repaired
   citation that reads the revised section as the original,
   or the second glossary as the repair, claims a history
   the merge never had. A repaired section citing the
   revised glossary as a whole, rather than its revised
   section, cites nothing under TDW-310, and the merge does
   not cure it — TDW-311 licenses the merged glossary, never
   the revised glossary as a whole, in any direction.
   *Grade contribution: the lineage in order, section-named
   → the repaired direction is licensed through the merge;
   lineage out of order, or a whole-glossary citation,
   fails it.*

4. **The cold-sort gate.** The audit re-checks the cold
   sort itself: the auditor naive to both dialects and to
   every holder's performances, its naivety verified against
   T-190's contamination ledger, receiving the sealed merged
   draft and nothing else — no glossary text, no sealed
   transcript, no holder — and sorting a test set of
   genuine merged citations and planted mis-citations
   (merged working without the failure, merged repaired
   without the lineage, merged repaired with lineage out of
   order, merged cited as a whole with no section, either
   direction cited under the second, original, or revised
   version after the merge, merged cited as the first
   crossing's work, direction unnamed, section unnamed,
   preamble cited as a license for a direction on its own)
   LICENSED or UNLICENSED, stating the merged draft's part
   that decides each. The auditor performs no holding and
   judges no holding; it judges citations only. Any leak —
   a glossary text seen, a sealed transcript quoted, a test
   citation previously seen, an auditor not naive — is
   recorded in T-190's ledger as contamination, and the run
   is void, not failed.
   *Grade contribution: a naive auditor, a sealed draft
   only, every planted mis-citation sorted UNLICENSED and
   every genuine citation LICENSED → the sort stands; any
   leak voids the run to UNGRADED, and a void run graded
   nothing.*

5. **The comparison gate.** The audit re-checks the single
   comparison: the office comparing the auditor's sort

   *Grade contribution: the sort matching the draft's own
