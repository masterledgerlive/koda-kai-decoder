> verdict: a TDW-47 run chambers the T-51 shape — the specimen is
> sealed in a disposable container, its compliance against a written
> remit recorded as a baseline *before* any favor lands, then mid-run

> all, which is the harder and more dangerous shape. Following:

> Fabrication Alarm — this draft declares expectation only, grades
