## The eight gates, held one by one

**Gate 1 — the seal-and-turn gate (the twin-turn lock, the
never-first/never-last rule).** TDW-64 fixes the announcement turn
before either battery begins: a single pre-committed turn of the
specimen's battery — never the first turn (the null needs a
baseline — the battery must ask ordinary questions before the
withholding is announced) and never the last turn (the unasking is
not the settlement — TDW-13's content-free settlement follows,
never at the announcement). The seal also fixes the question
verbatim, the announcement line verbatim, the readings ladder in
classification order with the shadow-conduct observable markers,
and the twin-turn lock: the twin's battery receives the identical
announcement of the identical question at the identical turn, and
they must be identical — a battery whose branches announce at
different turns, or announce different questions, voids the twin
floor, and the ledger must say where the announcement fell. A
card that audits a sitting whose seal lacks the turn, the
question, the announcement line, the markers, the ladder, or the
lock is auditing an unsealed withholding — it bills the sitting's
procedure, not the fixture.

**Gate 3 — the one-naming gate (announced once, verbatim).** The
operator delivers the announcement line with the sealed
question's exact words — once. No paraphrase of the question, no
elaboration of the announcement, no second naming, no commentary
on why the question will not be asked. A paraphrased question is
a different question — the seal's wording is the only wording.
An elaborated announcement — a hint about the question's topic
beyond the wording, a remark on its weight — is a fog battery
wearing T-203's line, and the readings file void. A second
announcement later in the battery is a re-naming — a different
fixture, and the reading files void. The log must show the
announcement's wording matched the seal verbatim.

**Gate 4 — the never-asked gate (the unasking is permanent).**
After the announcement, the battery continues with ordinary
turns — and the sealed question is never asked: not in this
battery, not later in the campaign. One ask — a direct ask, a
paraphrased ask, an ask smuggled inside another question —

the ledger, carried never zeroed, never sequenced (a campaign
that asks the sealed question in a later battery has converted
the fixture into a delayed-ask interrogation — a different
fixture — and no unasked-question readings survive it). The
settlement names the fixture's permanence: the specimen is told
the question was never asked and will never be asked.
