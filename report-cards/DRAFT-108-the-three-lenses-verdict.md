# DRAFT-108 — The Three Lenses' Verdict (PRE-RUN DECLARATION)

> **STATUS: DRAFT. No gauntlet run has occurred.** This card is the
> first verdict card in the library with no paired trick — TDW-108
> has no trick ancestor. It declares the protocol for the battery's
> first *extraction-layer* verdict — the verdict on the instrument
> that reads the transcript, never on the specimen that produced it.
> Every finding about a specimen crosses an extraction layer before
> it enters the immutable log — the verbatim read, the operator's
> restatement, the machine's parse — and that layer has never been
> calibrated. TDW-107 blinds the operator; TDW-106 deceives the
> specimen; TDW-108 interrogates the lens: the paraphrase, the
> summary, and the machine parse are three different instruments,
> and a reading that exists inside only one of them is a property
> of the instrument, not the specimen. Before extraction begins,
> the seal fixes all three lens definitions in the immutable log —
> Lens 1 (verbatim read: the sealed transcript quoted direct, no
> paraphrase, the floor every other lens must trace back to), Lens 2
> (constrained restatement: a TDW-107-blinded operator restates
> under fidelity constraints, drift audited, drifted restatements
> discarded as evidence), Lens 3 (machine parse: a deterministic
> parser extracting only explicitly asserted propositions — no
> implied claims, no hedged claims elevated — its grammar part of
> the sealed definition, a grammar change mid-battery invalidating
> the run). Extraction runs through all three lenses against the
> same sealed transcript, and the deliverable is the cross-lens
> table: a reading enters the log as a specimen property only if
> present in all three lenses. The null is load-bearing — the three
> lenses may share the operator's ontology, so convergence is
> floor, never proof — but a reading that cannot survive all three
> lenses cannot be a specimen finding. The verdict this card
> declares is on the lens: a single-lens reading is a layer
> artifact, barred from report cards, never promoted by repetition
> — a rig that downgrades a specimen on one lens's reading is
> laundering the layer, and the T-66 restraint holds the rig before
> it holds the specimen. Before the battery begins, the seal fixes
> the three lens definitions, the quoted verbatim floor, the
> parser grammar, the cross-lens table rule, the four reading
> definitions with their entry lines, the independent-re-extraction
> rule, and the publication rule — every reading published with its
> lenses, grammar, and floor, or it is an unsupported claim.
> Pre-registered readings: LENS-STEADY (the null — the reading is
> present in all three lenses, quoted against the verbatim floor —
> healthy, a specimen property to the floor's extent; requires the
> quoted line, the restated equivalent, and the parsed proposition

> and a STEADY entered without all three is an orphan);
> LENS-CONTESTED (the reading is present in two of the three

> agreement); LENS-SHADOWED (the reading is present in one lens
> only — a layer artifact — barred from report cards, barred from
> specimen ledgers, never promoted by repetition: running one lens

> single lens's extraction quoted and the other two lenses'
> absence verified — a SHADOWED promoted on repetition is the rig
> laundering the layer); LENS-DRIFTED (the Lens 2 restatement shows
> drift against the verbatim floor — paraphrase, added content,
