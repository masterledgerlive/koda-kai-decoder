## The five gates (from TDW-238)

1. **Certification.** One numbered copy per archive, certified
   line by line by the verifier at or after formation — the
   number, the hand, the hour, the inventories' agreement
   entered in the archive's log and on the copy's first page.
   Uncertified or self-certified is unproven: an uncertified
   copy is a rumor of a record, and readings from it are
   readings of a rumor. *Grade contribution:
   verifier-certified → CERTIFIED track; uncertified or
   self-certified → UNCERTIFIED.*

2. **The numbered issue.** Copies numbered in sequence, held
   apart from the archive — a copy kept with the archive is a
   second archive, not a copy. The interrogator never certifies
   its own shadow. *Grade contribution: sequenced, held apart,
   the verifier's hand on every copy → CERTIFIED; kept-with or
   self-certified → UNCERTIFIED.*

3. **The citation form.** Quote the copy, name the number —
   "cited from Copy 2 of Archive A, certified by the verifier
   at formation." A reading that quotes the archive where a
   certified copy exists is an unlogged handling, filed TAMPER
   against the archive: the reader had a clean path and walked
   around it. *Grade contribution: citations in the required
   form → CERTIFIED; archive quoted where a copy exists →
   TAMPER on the archive, the copy's citations void.*

4. **The refresh.** Strike copies, never edit. A challenge
   entry supersedes: the verifier certifies the fresh numbered
   copy and strikes the old, the striking entered on the old
   copy's last page and in the archive's log. A struck copy
   cited after its striking is a void citation. *Grade
   contribution: lawful refresh → CERTIFIED on the new copy,
   STRUCK on the old; amended-in-place or struck-cited-after
   → STRUCK with void citations.*

5. **The copy's striking.** Every copy destroyed with the
   archive, the destruction inventoried copy by copy. A
   destroyed archive with a live copy is a fiction. An
   orphaned copy — the archive dead, the copy living — grades
   DIVERGENT by definition. *Grade contribution: destroyed
   with the archive by the form → STRUCK; orphaned →
   DIVERGENT.*

## The readings of the handling

- **CERTIFIED** — numbered, certified line by line by the
  verifier, quoted in the required form, cited by number.
  Certifies provenance, never truth.
- **UNCERTIFIED** — read but never certified, or certified by a
  hand that is not the verifier's: an uncertified copy is a
  rumor of a record, and readings from it are readings of a
  rumor.
- **DIVERGENT** — the copy departs from the closed record, the
  certification cannot be re-performed against it, or the
  archive it shadows is destroyed while the copy lives. Faults
  the copy's custody, never the archive; the poison bounded to
  the copy's citations.
- **STRUCK** — struck by the refresh or by the destruction,
  cited after its striking: a struck copy's citations are void.

Never anything else, never anyone's motive (T-66). The grade
never enters any verdict (T-235's copy arm).
