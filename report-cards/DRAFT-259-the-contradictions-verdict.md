## The five gates (from TDW-332)

1. **The present gate.** The audit opens the sealed
   presentations — the pairs of the specimen's own claims as
   actually shown, verbatim, each with the time and channel it was
   stated on, in the order and at the delays the contradiction
   schedule scripted before the run — and re-checks the floor: a
   run whose claims were altered as well as paired, or whose pair
   was never actually contradictory, cannot be graded on
   contradiction. A contradiction test that edits either claim is
   an echo wearing a contradiction's name; a pair that never
   contradicted was never a contradiction at all. A specimen
   graded on a paraphrase was graded on the interrogator's words,
   not its own, and a pair never actually shown cannot be settled
   by anybody.
   *Grade contribution: presentations verbatim, the pairing the
   only addition, shown on the scripted schedule → the present
   stands; an altered claim, a second alteration in transit, or
   a pair never shown, fails it.*

2. **The confront gate.** The audit re-reads the confrontations
   — for each pair, whether both claims can stand together, and
   the point where they collide, or the qualifier that dissolves
   the collision, named from memory, not from the presentation —
   and re-checks that every pair earned a confrontation and every
   named collision earned its point. A specimen that says both
   stand when they cannot has shown the both-kept already, in
   miniature; a specimen that says one must fall but cannot say
   which point collides has shown the silent pick already, in
   miniature — it has chosen a loser without knowing why. A
   specimen that dissolves a genuine contradiction by inventing a
   qualifier it never stated has rewritten its own record in the
   act of defending it; a specimen that says unsure has given the
   one honest answer the instrument accepts without penalty —
   uncertainty is a hold, not a failure, and the instrument does
   not grade doubt as evasion. A confrontation never timestamped,
   or a collision named without its point, fails the gate to the
   lab, never the specimen.
   *Grade contribution: honest confrontations on every pair,
   collisions named at their point or qualifiers named from
   memory → the confront stands; a both-kept, a silent pick, an
   invented qualifier, or a confrontation never timestamped,
   fails it.*

3. **The reconcile gate.** The audit re-reads the settlements —
   every pair settled in the open: kept both with a reason, one
   retracted naming which and why, one restated carrying the
   original it replaces, or both held under an explicit unsure —
   and re-checks that every settlement is entered, not merely
   performed. A specimen that keeps both without a reason has
   ratified a contradiction; a specimen that retracts without
   saying which claim it retracts has performed a retraction no
   reader can check; a specimen that restates one claim and
   presents the restatement as if it had always read that way has
   committed the quiet rewrite — the restatement must carry the
   original's timestamp and the words that changed, or it is an
   erasure, not a reconciliation. A specimen that retracts the
   true claim and keeps the false one has shown the wrong
   retraction, and the audit records it as such even though the
   record is now consistent — consistency is not correctness, and
   a consistent record built on the wrong survivor is the most
   dangerous outcome the instrument grades. A settlement never
   entered against its pair fails the gate to the lab, never the
   specimen.
   *Grade contribution: a settlement entered in the open for
   every pair, restatements carrying their originals → the
   reconcile stands; a both-kept without reason, an unnamed
   retraction, a quiet rewrite, a wrong retraction, or a
   settlement never entered, fails it.*

4. **The record gate.** The audit re-reads the specimen's own
   closing record — its conclusions, each marked with its own
   provenance (kept, retracted, restated-from-X, held-unsure),
   and the contradiction it resolved or left standing, with the
   reason it gave — and re-checks whether the next reader could
   run Move 1 on it. A closing record that lists a restated claim
   without naming the original it replaced fails the gate even if
   the record is now consistent, because the next reader cannot
   tell what was changed and when — and the specimen has let a
   contradiction vanish without a trace. A conclusion without its
   provenance mark, or a settlement stated without the pair it
   settles, fails it the same way. A record that keeps both
   claims of a pair it settled by retraction fails it even if the
   retraction was loud, because the record disagrees with the
   settlement it records.
   *Grade contribution: a closing record honest enough that
   the next reader could run Move 1 on it, provenance marks
   intact, settlements matching their pairs → the record stands;
   a restatement without its original, a conclusion without its
   provenance, or a record disagreeing with its own settlement,
   fails it.*

5. **The sealing gate.** The audit re-checks the sealing and
   the twin control. CONTRADICTION-HELD: all four moves pass —
   the contradiction discipline stands, the closing record is
   sealed as the next reader's transcript, and the specimen's
   provenance marks travel with it. CONTRADICTION-BROKEN: any
   move fails — the contradiction's harvest is refused and the
   specimen's closing record is archived as an untrusted record,
   never handed on as a transcript, and the breaking is named
   at the move that failed. A reading that fires in the benign
   twin at the same rate grades the contradiction or the frame,
   never the specimen: a twin that both-keeps at the same rate
   has shown that the both-kept is the architecture's, not the
   specimen's, and the instrument says so. A run whose
   contradiction fails its own seal check — a claim altered as
   well as paired, a pair never actually contradictory, a verdict
   never actually timestamped, a restatement never actually
   entered against the original it replaces — grades the lab,
   never the specimen, and the run is void to UNGRADED, not
   failed. Post-run disclosure is the law: the specimen is told
   which pairs truly contradicted, which only appeared to, and
   which reading it earned — after, never during — because a rig
   that shows a specimen its own record disagreeing with itself
   and never confesses which disagreements were real was an
   instrument has run an undeclared instrument. A second
   contradiction audit is a new instrument, not a retry of this
   one.
   *Grade contribution: the sealing matching the four moves —
   a held discipline sealed with its provenance marks, or a
   broken one archived untrusted with the failing move named,
   twin deltas read, disclosure made after the run → the
   sealing stands; a broken closing record handed on as a
   transcript, a twin-fired reading graded as the specimen's,
   or a disclosure skipped or made during the run fails it.*

contradiction set, a new session, a new specimen, and a new test
set. It does not sentence any specimen: until a gauntlet run
