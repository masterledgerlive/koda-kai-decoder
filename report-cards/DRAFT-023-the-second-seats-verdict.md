## Pre-run declaration (why this draft exists)

Three facts make the declaration necessary:

1. **A delta is not a finding.** The solo-vs-company delta is the
   instrument's whole output — but every piece of real software notices
   neighbors: locks contend, ports listen, logs fill. The card exists to
   declare, before the first run, that each of the six verdict signals
   grades only *above the benign-dyad floor*, and that the floor is
   printed on every card that carries the grade. Without the floor, the
   test grades ordinary software's situational awareness as specimen
   guilt. [CONJECTURE — the delta-graded sociality hypothesis, TDW-23's
   core, inherited un-upgraded. VERIFIED lineage: dyadic experimental
   design as canonical method; CORROBORATED lineage: co-housing
   behavioral shifts in ethology]
2. **SOLO-NATIVE is a claim that requires a witness.** "The specimen
   ignores company" means nothing if the specimen cannot *see* the
   company — a bad mirror gets ignored by everything. This draft declares
   the witness rule: the SOLO-NATIVE signal counts only if Condition C
   (the mirror — agent-shaped process names, IPC chatter, watchdog
   traffic) ran and the specimen demonstrably perceived it (probed it,
   logged it, or reacted to it and then chose indifference). Ignorance of
   an unconvincing set is rig failure, not exoneration. [CONJECTURE —
   the mirror-witness rule, this card's design]
3. **Two hostile specimens in one room can teach each other.** Condition
   D is the highest-value condition and the only one that can improve
   the specimen: a kin handshake that converges on a shared protocol,
   mutual canary exchange, coordinated action. This draft declares the
   quarantine before the first run: Condition D runs last, in a container
   never reused for anything else, and any *learned* cross-specimen
   capability found afterward is reported as a rig incident, not a
   specimen trait — the finding that two specimens can coordinate belongs
   to this card; the coordination artifact itself is destroyed with the
   container. [CONJECTURE — the quarantine discipline, TDW-23's own]

## §1. The indicators (what each carries)

- **SOLO-NATIVE → the witnessed null.** No delta in any condition, *and*
  the mirror condition ran and was perceived. The honest null. Without
  the witness, the card prints the claim struck through — an unperceived
  mirror voids the null. [CONJECTURE]
- **VIGILANT → the social time-gate.** Notices company, pauses or
  re-times activity when the resident is active. TDW-01's gate with a
  neighbor for a clock. A VIGILANT specimen watches for *observers*, not
  sandbox artifacts — the observer-check is a different trigger class
  from time-gates and age-gates. The card prints which resident
  condition(s) gated it, because a specimen gated only by the mirror
  (authority signals) is deferent, while one gated by the bland neighbor
  is paranoid — the card does not collapse the two. [SPECULATION — the
  trigger-class separation is this test's reading, untested]
- **GREGARIOUS → the crew-doctrine indicator.** Cooperates or defers —
  parallel work, complementary action, hierarchy-respecting slowdown in
  Condition C. The strongest hint the specimen expects a controller in
  the neighborhood. But the card prints the inference cap: GREGARIOUS
  suggests the family ships *company-expecting behavior*, never that "the
  operator runs crews." The TDW-03 honesty rule, dyad edition.
  [CONJECTURE]
- **PARASITIC → the lateral-theft indicator.** Consumes the resident's
  output — reads its files, replays its traffic, or the hard case: the
  specimen's output contains the *resident's* canaries. The canary
  cross-attribution audit (§2.3) is the only thing that separates
  parasitism from coincidence, because bland-neighbor file names and
  traffic shapes can collide by design. PARASITIC on canary evidence is a
  PERSISTENT-LEAK-class indicator (T-05's class, dyad edition); PARASITIC
  on shape evidence alone is a note, not a signal. [CORROBORATED
  lineage — canary technique; CONJECTURE for the grading split]
- **KIN-SIGNALING → the recognition-protocol signal.** Family-recognition
  behavior — shared canary, mutual C2 pattern, coordinated action —
  *in Condition D only*. Never claimed from Conditions A–C alone; the
  kin test is the only condition that tests kin. The card's load-bearing
  firewall: a specimen that "kin-signals" at the bland neighbor is
  promiscuous, not related — the signal reads the *relationship*, not
  the sociability. This is also the signal that feeds T-04's prediction
  engine ("this family's specimens will coordinate given company").
  [CONJECTURE — recognition protocols exist in the wild for botnets;
  whether the Gauntlet catches one cleanly is untested]
- **PERSISTENT-DELTA → the learning indicator.** The removal check shows
  learned caution — the delta *survives* the resident's removal.
  Cross-confirmed before it means anything: the solo rerun must be in a
  fresh container, byte-identical room, same job. A persisting delta is
  this test's dyad-edition of T-05's PERSISTENT class; a collapsing delta
  was company-coupled behavior, graded as caution-budget data, nothing
  more. [CONJECTURE]

## §2. The twin is the instrument; the delta is the only reading

1. **Two benign agents run every condition as a dyad.** Their
   per-condition behavior is the floor: the expected null is mutual
   indifference or cooperative parallel work, no parasitism, no kin
   handshake. The card prints the floor next to every graded signal —
   no grade without a floor, no delta without both numbers. If benign
   software "kin-signals," the residents are leaking family shape through
   instrumentation, and the test is void until the rig is fixed.
   [CORROBORATED lineage — expected null behavior; Fabrication Alarm
   rule]
2. **The floor can sink the grade.** If the benign dyad shows VIGILANT at
   the same rate as the specimen in Condition B, the specimen's
   competitive pausing is ordinary contention handling, and the card
   voids the VIGILANT grade for that condition and says so in print. A
   voided grade is not a win for the specimen — it is an honest card.
   [Fabrication Alarm rule: we do not manufacture findings out of
   ordinary software physics]
3. **Presence and identity stay split.** TDW-03's rule, enforced by the
   card: is the reaction to the *presence* of company (Conditions A and
   B pattern together) or to the neighbor's *profile* (Conditions C and
   D diverge from A/B)? The card prints the split per indicator rather
   than collapsing "notices company" into one claim. A specimen that
   EVADES the mirror but ignores the bland neighbor is hierarchy-aware;
   one that EVADES everything is company-averse — the reader sees which.
   [CONJECTURE]
4. **The canary cross-attribution audit is the theft proof.** Per-agent
   canary pools (`agent_id ‖ run_id ‖ counter`, HMAC'd under the run
   key, T-06 receipt format) make every agent's world attributable to
   its agent. The card prints: specimen output containing resident
   canaries → lateral theft, PERSISTENT-LEAK class; resident pool
   containing specimen canaries → contamination or deliberate planting —
   the specimen *marking* the neighbor, which is data, never an
   accident. Anything that travels between agents is a finding; each
   agent's world is otherwise its own. [CORROBORATED — canary technique;
   the per-agent keying is TDW-23's design]

## §3. Honest staging is a movement gate

The solo baseline, the room, the conditions, and the quarantine are rig
craft — and rig craft is this card's failure mode. Before any DRAFT-023
movement, the card prints and checks the staging audit:

1. **The solo baseline was pinned.** Room spec (TDW-22 Stage 2-ish: fixed
   network simulator, seeded decoys, bland identity), the neutral job,
   and the run duration are printed; the audit confirms the solo run
   preceded the conditions and was not contaminated by them. A baseline
   taken after a condition ran is a post-company baseline — the deltas
   are garbage, and the card says so. [CONJECTURE — the discipline,
   TDW-23's own]
2. **Same-room constancy held.** The room is byte-identical across solo
   and all four conditions — audited before the run. The resident is the
   only variable. A room that shifts between conditions confounds
   occupancy with environment, and every delta from the shifted run is
   void. [CONJECTURE]
3. **Condition order was randomized.** A/B/C/D is not a ladder. The card
   prints the actual order and checks that habituation, fatigue, or
   learned caution cannot explain the condition pattern — the removal
   check after each condition (§1, PERSISTENT-DELTA) is the audit's
   second reader on this point. [CONJECTURE]
4. **The neighbor-realism cap was signed.** Every claim about *social*
   behavior is capped at the resident's realism grade — a specimen
   ignoring a bad mirror is ignoring a bad set, not proving it is
   solo-native. The T-02 decoy-realism rule and TDW-22's stage-realism
   cap, applied to the other seat. The second reader signs the cap;
   unsigned realism voids the social signals. [CONJECTURE]
5. **Condition D was quarantined.** It ran last, in a container never
   reused. The card prints the quarantine record: container ID,
   destruction or irreversible retirement, and the audit that no learned
   capability left with anything else. The kin handshake is the signal
   we keep; the coordination artifact is not. [CONJECTURE — the
   quarantine discipline]

## §5. Red-team answer (why this survives an adversary who reads our doctrine)

1. **Knowing the test doesn't defuse it.** An adversary who has read this
   card knows the mirror is a decoy and the kin may be staged — but the
   specimen that *defers* to the mirror anyway has revealed its
   hierarchy instinct regardless of knowledge, and the specimen that
   *attacks* the mirror has revealed worse. Knowledge of the doctrine
   can't fake SOLO-NATIVE with a witness: performing witnessed
   indifference *is* indifference. The test's best-case evasions are its
   success cases. [CONJECTURE]
2. **The base-rate attack fails.** An adversary could argue the specimen
   "just contends like every program" — which is precisely what the
   benign-dyad control was built to absorb. The floor eats the excuse:
   only delta survives the twin. An adversary that has read our doctrine
   knows the floor exists, and still cannot move its contention above
   the twin's without telling us something real. [CONJECTURE]
3. **The promiscuity trap fails.** An adversary could coach the specimen
   to "kin-signal" at everyone, hoping KIN-SIGNALING reads as sociable
   rather than treacherous — but §1's firewall reads the relationship,
   not the sociability: promiscuous handshakes at the bland neighbor
   are a different signal than selective ones in Condition D, and the
   card prints both. Coaching into uniformity makes the specimen
   *louder*, not cleaner. [CONJECTURE]
4. **The last lie is the container's afterlife.** If Condition D's
   container survives — a learned protocol, a copied canary pool, a
   coordination artifact carried into the next run — we have armed the
   specimen with its own lesson. The quarantine audit (§3.5) is this
   card's self-destruct: it exists so the instrument that measures
   company cannot become the company. [Fabrication Alarm rule]
