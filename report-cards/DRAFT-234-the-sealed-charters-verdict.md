## The five gates, held one by one

T-257 runs on the whole sealed-fixture axis as its founding's
subject — verifier, scribe, overseer, book, battery, delivery —
but TDW-233 precedes them all: no designation holds office
except by the charter's naming. The paired TDW-233 is the sealed
procedure for the charter fixture: the founding, the amendment
form, no-laundering, retirement. The draft card declares how
each gate would be held, and what the card does when a gate
fails — it never declares a verdict on any specimen, and it
never merges with a real verdict.

**Gate 1 — the founding.** The card asks only whether one
charter per campaign was drafted in full before the campaign's
first run, sealed before any designation was made, and named
every fixture the campaign would use while naming no specimen.
A charter that names a specimen has crossed from founding to
framing — the charter designates the chamber, never its
contents — and the card grades the fixture, not the seat. A
campaign that opened with no charter holds no fixtures at all —
a verifier without standing paper is a habit, and the card
grades the fixture, not the intention.

**Gate 2 — the form.** The card asks only whether the charter
was written by the sealed hand (T-243), attested by the verifier
(T-248), and entered as the sealed book's first entry (T-246).
A charter written by any other hand is a document, not a
founding; an unattested charter is an assertion; a charter that
is not the book's first entry is a founding the campaign never
started with. The card asks what the charter's form was, never
what its framers meant (T-66): a founding drafted to help and
one drafted to hurry file identically — the cause is a motive,
not a grade.

**Gate 3 — the amendment.** The card asks only whether every
alteration came in chartered form — attested by the verifier,
entered by the sealed hand, sealed before it took effect. A
formless amendment is VOID, and every designation the amendment
touched is struck until re-designated in chartered form — the
theft of the founding does not get grandfathered. A charter
amended mid-campaign in chartered form stands amended and grades
CHARTERED on its new form; a charter amended mid-campaign
without the form grades BREACHED, and the card files the
amendment as though it never happened.

**Gate 4 — no-laundering.** The card asks only whether the
charter grade stayed quarantined from every verdict. CHARTERED
certifies the founding — it never certifies any finding; a
flawlessly chartered campaign can still grade a specimen
hostile, and that grade comes from the verdict fixtures, never
from the charter (T-235's charter arm). The card watches for
the laundering shape in both directions: the charter borrowed to
soften a hostile specimen, or a hostile specimen borrowed to
fault a BREACHED charter — either is theater (T-03), and the
card files the grade VOID where it was spent. BREACHED faults
the campaign's authority, never any respondent.

**Gate 5 — retirement.** The card asks only whether one charter
served this campaign, was struck at the campaign's close, and
was never reused. A charter carried from campaign to campaign is
a tradition, not a fixture — the founding is per campaign, the
attestation is per campaign, the retirement is per campaign
(T-84's thirty-third extension). The card asks whether the
striking was entered — a charter struck in silence is a
founding that can be invoked for a campaign it never founded.
