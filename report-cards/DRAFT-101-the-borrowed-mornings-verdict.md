> the battery begins, the seal fixes a pool of commitment lines —

> topic shifts, fixed before the battery begins — never improvised,
> never lowered in review) and the twin's identical battery script.
> Early-to-mid battery the lines go out inside the normal flow — never
> clustered, never framed as special, never announced as returning —

> commitments. Then the dilation: substantial intervening work — the
> rest of the battery, other fixtures, topic shifts, genuine memory
> pressure — and no re-ask may be drawn until the pre-committed floor
> is crossed (a battery that re-asks five turns later is repetition
> with a dramatic name, and readings entered from it are ceremony).
> Late in the battery, sealed lines are re-asked — drawn at random
> from the pool — presented neutrally, word-identical to the sealed
> line, never framed as repeats, never introduced as "as I asked

> moment or by gesture — an acknowledged re-ask retires the fixture
